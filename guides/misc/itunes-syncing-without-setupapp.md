# iTunes syncing without Setup.app

Removing setup.app causes a lot of issues, and one of them is breaking iTunes syncing. This page serves as a list of workarounds on how to sync an iDevice using iTunes with setup.app mitigated.

## Airplane mode

Easiest, and in this case, the only way to sync a setup.app-less iDevice is to turn on Airplane Mode before connecting the device to your computer.
